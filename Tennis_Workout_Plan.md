## ⭐ Daily Elbow Protocol (~7 min, EVERY day)

Both tennis elbow (lateral epicondyle) and golf elbow (medial) respond best to **eccentric loading** — slow, controlled lowering under resistance. The evidence (Tyler et al.) is built on **daily** loading: tendon remodeling responds to frequency, not intensity. Tie this to a fixed daily cue — e.g. every evening while watching TV — so it happens whether or not you trained that day. Expect **6–8 weeks** of consistent daily work before full results. Don't stop when the elbows feel okay; the effect is cumulative.

1. **Tyler Twist** (tennis elbow): Hold FlexBar vertically, affected arm at bottom palm down. Top hand twists the bar, then slowly let the bottom hand untwist — **3 × 15**. · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=tyler+twist+flexbar+tennis+elbow+shorts)
2. **Reverse Tyler Twist** (golf elbow): Same but affected arm palm up — **3 × 15**. · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=reverse+tyler+twist+flexbar+golfers+elbow+shorts)
3. **Pronation/Supination**: Hold a light DB by **one end**, elbow at 90°. Slowly rotate palm up then palm down, full range — **2 × 15 each direction**. *Start with a short lever (grip near the weighted end); lengthen the lever before adding weight.* · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=dumbbell+wrist+pronation+supination+exercise+shorts)
4. **Wrist Extensor Stretch**: Arm straight, palm down, gently pull fingers toward you — **3 × 30 sec**. · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=wrist+extensor+stretch+tennis+elbow+shorts)
5. **Finger Extension**: Loop a rubber band around all five fingers, slowly splay open against resistance — **3 × 20**. Balances the constant gripping load on the forearm flexors. · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=finger+extension+rubber+band+exercise+shorts)

### Symptom Gate (applies to elbow work AND all loaded gripping)
- **Pain ≤ 3/10 during, and no worse the next morning → proceed / progress.**
- **Pain > 3–4/10, or worse the next morning → hold load where it is, or drop 20%, until it settles.**
- Sharp, pinpoint, or radiating pain → stop that movement for the day.
- Mild dull ache during eccentrics is normal and expected.

## 🎾 Pre-Match Warm-Up (~5 min, on court before a class or match)

Primes movement, rotation, the racquet arm, and the elbow — without fatiguing anything.

1. **Achilles pogos + easy skips — 45 sec.** Raise the heart rate, prime calves and feet.
2. **Leg swings — 45 sec.** Front-to-back ×10 and lateral ×10 each leg.
3. **Walking lunge + reach/rotate — 45 sec.** 4–5 each side, twist toward the front leg (hips + thoracic spine).
4. **Arm circles → cross-body swings → wrist rolls — 30 sec.** Wakes the shoulder and forearm gently.
5. **Forearm/elbow prep — 20–30 sec.** Wrist flexion/extension + circles; a few **light** FlexBar twists with the **yellow** bar (easy, for blood flow — *not* the slow daily eccentrics); a couple of grip squeeze-and-spreads.
6. **Lateral shuffles + 2 split steps + 2 short accelerations — 45 sec.** Move in the planes you'll play in.
7. **Shadow swings — 60 sec.** 5 forehands, 5 backhands, 5 serve motions, building slow → full speed. Grooves the pattern and primes the serve shoulder.

Then go into your mini-rally / warm-up hit and **build swing pace gradually — never first-ball-full-pace.** That's your #1 on-court elbow protector.

*Rib note: while a rib is healing, keep the lunge rotation (3) and shadow serves (7) gentle and pain-free.*

## Day 1: Lower Body Power + Lateral Quickness

**Goal:** Build hip power and lateral explosiveness for split-step, first-step quickness, and court coverage.
*(Numbers below = Week 1–2 starting dose. See Ramp-Up Schedule for progression.)*

### Warm-Up (10 min)
- **Forearm prep (KB day):** light pronation/supination + wrist extensor stretch, 1 easy set each — primes the forearms *before* heavy gripping
- Lateral shuffles: 2 min (two targets ~10 ft apart, stay low, push off outside foot, don't cross feet) — [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=lateral+shuffle+drill+tennis+shorts)
- Child's pose rocks: 10 reps (thoracic and hip flexor opener) — [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=childs+pose+rock+back+mobility+shorts)
- 90/90 pop-ups: 10 reps (sit in 90/90, lift front knee to switch sides — targets hip IR) — [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=90+90+hip+popups+switch+mobility+shorts)
- 90/90 IR bias hold: 45 sec each side (lean torso toward front shin, let rear hip sink) — [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=90+90+hip+internal+rotation+stretch+shorts)
- Lateral leg swings: 10 each leg — [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=lateral+leg+swings+warm+up+shorts)
- Lateral band walks: 2 × 15 each direction (glute activation — primes lateral push-off) — [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=lateral+band+walk+glute+exercise+shorts)
- Split step practice: 20 reps (jump → land in athletic stance, absorb) — [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=tennis+split+step+drill+shorts)

### Main Work (~25 min at starting load)

**1. Kettlebell Swings — 3 × 12** *(target 4 × 15)* · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=kettlebell+swing+proper+form+shorts)
Hip hinge power. Drive hips hard. Brace at the top. Relaxed grip — just enough tension to control the bell.

**2. Bulgarian Split Squat — 2 × 8 each leg** *(target 3 × 8, heavier)* · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=bulgarian+split+squat+proper+form+shorts)
Rear foot on TRX or bench. **Start bodyweight — it's plenty hard at first; add dumbbells or a KB once you own the balance and depth.** Builds single-leg strength for lunge mechanics.

**3. Lateral Bounds (Skater Jumps) — 2 × 4 each direction** *(target 3 × 6)* · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=skater+jumps+lateral+bound+form+shorts)
Push off one foot sideways, land on the opposite foot only. Pause 1 sec to absorb and balance, then push back. If single-leg landing feels unstable, touch the other foot down as a kickstand. **Full rest between reps — quality, not fatigue.**

**4. TRX Squat + Explosive Jump — 3 × (3 slow + 1 jump)** *(target 4 sets)* · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=trx+squat+jump+exercise+shorts)
Handles at sides, body at ~45°. Do 3 slow reps (3 sec down, pause at bottom, drive up), then on the 4th rep explode into a jump. Land softly, absorb through hips, reset.

**5. Calf Raises — plantar-fascia loading — 3 × 10 each foot, single-leg, ~30 lb** · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=heel+raise+towel+under+toes+plantar+fasciitis+shorts)
*(2×/week — Day 1 & Day 3, for your plantar/metatarsal history.)* Single-leg heel raise on a step holding ~30 lb, with a **rolled towel under the toes** so the big toe is bent up — this loads the plantar fascia through the windlass mechanism, the most evidence-backed loading fix for plantar fasciitis. Slow tempo: 3 sec up, 2 sec hold at the top, 3 sec down. **Foot symptom gate applies** — lingering morning heel pain means hold or drop load. *(Already at this load — progress by adding reps, then weight, when it feels easy.)*

### Finisher (~5 min)
Agility ladder: **3 rounds** *(target 5)* of your choice (lateral icky shuffle, in-in-out-out, two feet each box forward). Rest 30 sec between rounds. — [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=agility+ladder+drills+tennis+footwork+shorts)

## Day 2: Upper Body Strength + Rotational Power

**Goal:** Build the kinetic chain for serve and groundstroke power without stressing the elbows. Emphasis on pulling strength and scapular stability.
*(Numbers below = Week 1–2 starting dose.)*

### Warm-Up (10 min)
- Child's pose rocks: 10 reps — [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=childs+pose+rock+back+mobility+shorts)
- 90/90 pop-ups: 10 reps — [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=90+90+hip+popups+switch+mobility+shorts)
- 90/90 IR bias hold: 45 sec each side — [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=90+90+hip+internal+rotation+stretch+shorts)
- Band pull-aparts: 2 × 15 — [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=band+pull+apart+exercise+form+shorts)
- Shoulder CARs (controlled articular rotations): 5 each arm — [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=shoulder+CARs+controlled+articular+rotations+shorts)
- Arm swings: 10 forward, 10 cross-body each arm — [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=arm+swings+shoulder+warm+up+shorts)
- Wrist circles: 30 sec — [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=wrist+circles+warm+up+shorts)
- Band external rotation: 1–2 × 12 each arm (rotator cuff primer) — [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=band+external+rotation+rotator+cuff+exercise+shorts)

### Main Work (~25 min at starting load)

**1. Pull-Up Progression (band-assisted) — 2 × 5** *(target 3 × 6–8)* · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=band+assisted+pull+up+progression+shorts)
*(Needs a pull-up bar + assist bands.)* Vertical pull — complements the DB row instead of repeating a horizontal pull, and builds toward your unassisted pull-up. **Use a neutral or pronated grip and stay inside the symptom gate** — pull-ups load the elbow tendons, so back off volume if they flare. Work the levels; move up when 3×6–8 feels solid:
- *Level 1:* Dead hang + scapular pulls — hang, then shrug shoulders down/back without bending the elbows (grip + scap control)
- *Level 2:* Thick-band assisted pull-up — most assistance
- *Level 3:* Lighter-band assisted — less help as you get stronger
- *Level 4:* Eccentric-only — jump or step to the top, lower slowly 3–5 sec (big strength gains, tendon-friendly)
- *Level 5:* Full unassisted pull-up

> **Frequency tip (the real key to your first pull-up):** one hard set a week won't get you there fast. Practice the progression **2–3× more across the week** — a few easy sets of dead hangs, scap pulls, or slow eccentrics, always stopping well short of failure ("grease the groove"). It's low-fatigue and joint-friendly, so it won't interfere with your other sessions — same principle as the daily elbow work. Keep grip relaxed and stay under the symptom gate.

**2. Single-Arm Dumbbell Row — 2 × 10 each arm** *(target 3 × 10)* · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=single+arm+dumbbell+row+form+shorts)
Knee on bench or TRX assist. Moderate-heavy. Pulling strength transfers to topspin groundstrokes.

**3. Dumbbell Shoulder Press — 2 × 10** *(target 3 × 10)* · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=dumbbell+shoulder+press+proper+form+shorts)
Seated or standing. Moderate weight. Overhead stability for serve and overhead smash.

**4. Medicine Ball Rotational Wall Throw — 3 × 6 each side** *(target 4 × 8)* · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=medicine+ball+rotational+wall+throw+tennis+shorts)
Stand sideways 3–4 ft from a solid wall. Load the hip and shoulder, throw through rotation, catch and repeat. **This is your power exercise.** Drive from the ground up — legs → hips → core → arm.

**5. Hammer Curls — 2 × 8, MODERATE** *(target 3 × 8)* · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=hammer+curl+proper+form+shorts)
Neutral grip (palms facing in), slow 3-sec lower, full extension at the bottom. Targets the brachialis. **Keep load moderate, not heavy** — heavy loaded elbow flexion can provoke the tendons while they're healing. Progress only if the symptom gate stays green.

**6. AxisBoard Single-Leg Balance — 2 × 20–30 sec each foot** *(progress: 45 sec → eyes closed → single-leg RDL reach)* · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=single+leg+balance+board+exercise+shorts)
*(2×/week — Day 2 & Day 4.)* Barefoot or socks, soft knee — find a "quiet foot" and minimize wobble. Trains the foot intrinsics and ankle stabilizers that guard your plantar/metatarsal history and sharpen court balance. Low-impact, so it's safe for the feet. Progress only when steady: eyes open → eyes closed → add a slow single-leg RDL reach (hinge and tap the floor ahead, stand back up).

## Day 3: Full Body Explosive + Agility

**Goal:** Peak power, multi-directional explosiveness, and neuromuscular speed. Closest to on-court demands.
*(This is the highest-load day — it starts the most reduced. Numbers below = Week 1–2 starting dose.)*

### Warm-Up (10 min)
- **Forearm prep (KB day):** light pronation/supination + wrist extensor stretch, 1 easy set each
- Fast feet in place: 2 min (alternate feet as fast as possible, stay on the balls of your feet) — [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=fast+feet+drill+in+place+shorts)
- Child's pose rocks: 10 reps — [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=childs+pose+rock+back+mobility+shorts)
- 90/90 pop-ups: 10 reps — [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=90+90+hip+popups+switch+mobility+shorts)
- 90/90 IR bias hold: 45 sec each side — [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=90+90+hip+internal+rotation+stretch+shorts)
- World's greatest stretch: 5 each side — [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=worlds+greatest+stretch+shorts)
- Arm circles + torso rotations: 60 sec — [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=arm+circles+torso+rotation+warm+up+shorts)

### Main Work (~25 min at starting load)

**1. KB Clean + Press — 2 × 5 each side** *(target 3 × 5)* · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=kettlebell+clean+and+press+form+shorts)
Link the hip drive from the clean directly into overhead press. Power chain integration.

**2. Broad Jump — 2 × 4** *(target 4 × 5)* · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=broad+jump+standing+long+jump+form+shorts)
Jump forward as far as possible. Full hip extension at takeoff, land soft on both feet, absorb through hips. Walk back, **reset completely between reps. Train quality, not fatigue.**

**3. Medicine Ball Overhead Slam — 2 × 6** *(target 3 × 8)* · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=medicine+ball+overhead+slam+form+shorts)
Reach tall, drive the ball straight down with full body. Builds power and releases shoulder tension.

**4. Push-Up (progress through levels) — 2 × 8** *(target 3 × 8)* · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=push+up+proper+form+progression+shorts)
Move up a level when 3×8 feels solid with good form:
- *Level 1:* Incline (hands on counter or bench) — start here if needed
- *Level 2:* Knee push-up — full range, shorter lever
- *Level 3:* Full push-up — hands and toes, rigid plank throughout
- *Level 4:* Feet-elevated (feet on TRX or chair)
- *Level 5:* Explosive — push hard enough that hands leave the floor, land soft, reset between reps
- *Level 6:* TRX Atomic — explosive push-up with feet in TRX handles + knee tuck

**5. Lateral Shuffle + Split Step Sequence — 3 rounds** *(Weeks 1–2 only)* · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=tennis+lateral+shuffle+split+step+drill+shorts)
Two targets 10–15 ft apart. Shuffle laterally, hit split step at each end, shuffle back. 20 sec on, 10 sec rest. Pure tennis movement. **This is your conditioning piece in Weeks 1–2 — once the finisher below comes in (Week 3+), drop this; they're the same stimulus and you don't need both.**

**6. Calf Raises — plantar-fascia loading — 3 × 10 each foot, single-leg, ~30 lb** · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=heel+raise+towel+under+toes+plantar+fasciitis+shorts)
*(2×/week — Day 1 & Day 3.)* Same as Day 1: single-leg heel raise on a step holding ~30 lb, rolled towel under the toes, slow 3-2-3 tempo. Watch the foot symptom gate.

### Finisher — *introduced Week 3+, replaces main-work #5*
3 rounds: 10 KB swings → 5 broad jumps → 10 split steps. No rest within rounds, 30 sec between. *Build from 1 round (Week 3) to 3 rounds (Weeks 7–8). Skip entirely in Weeks 1–2 — run #5 instead while you adapt. From Week 3 this is the conditioning piece and #5 comes out.*

## Day 4: Core, Stability + Hip Mobility

**Goal:** Deep core stability, rotational control, hip mobility. Lower-intensity day — active recovery that still builds. *(Minimal reduction — this is your easy day. Mostly run as written.)*

### Warm-Up (5 min)
- Foam roll or lacrosse ball: calves, IT band, thoracic spine (2 min) — [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=foam+rolling+calves+it+band+thoracic+spine+shorts)
- Cat-cow: 10 reps — [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=cat+cow+stretch+shorts)
- Thread the needle: 10 each side — [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=thread+the+needle+stretch+shorts)

### Main Work (30 min)

**1. TRX Plank Variations — 2 × 30 sec** *(target 3 × 30 sec)* · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=trx+plank+variations+shorts)
Hands in TRX handles (or feet). Progress: standard → hip drop → pike. Unstable surface recruits far more core.

**2. Suitcase Carry — 3 × 30–40 sec each side** · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=suitcase+carry+exercise+form+shorts)
Hold one heavy DB or KB at your side, walk tall and braced, resisting any lean toward the weight. Anti-lateral-flexion core plus grip and posture — a vector the plank doesn't cover. **Relaxed grip, moderate load, stay under the symptom gate** — it's a gripping exercise, so ease off if the elbows complain.

**3. Band Chop (High to Low) — 3 × 12 each side** · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=band+chop+high+to+low+core+exercise+shorts)
Anchor band high. Rotate and pull diagonally across body, low and opposite. Mimics the forehand finish. Core rotation under load.

**4. Pallof Press (Kneeling) — 3 × 12 each side** · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=kneeling+pallof+press+form+shorts)
Kneeling increases demand on hip stability. Press, hold 2 sec, return slowly.

**5. KB Windmill — 3 × 5 each side** · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=kettlebell+windmill+proper+form+shorts)
Light KB. Hip hinge to the side, KB pressed overhead. Demands shoulder stability, hip mobility, and lateral core control simultaneously.

**6. AxisBoard Single-Leg Balance — 2 × 20–30 sec each foot** *(progress: 45 sec → eyes closed → single-leg RDL reach)* · [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=single+leg+balance+board+exercise+shorts)
*(2×/week — Day 2 & Day 4.)* Barefoot or socks, soft knee, quiet foot. Foot intrinsics + ankle stability for your plantar/metatarsal history and court balance. Eyes open → eyes closed → single-leg RDL reach.

**7. Hip Mobility Circuit — 2 rounds**
- 90/90 stretch: 60 sec each side — [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=90+90+hip+stretch+shorts)
- Pigeon pose: 60 sec each side — [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=pigeon+pose+hip+stretch+shorts)
- Adductor rock-back: 10 reps — [▶](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=adductor+rock+back+stretch+shorts)

Tight hips force compensation up the chain — tennis elbow is often partly a hip mobility problem.

## 📈 8-Week Ramp-Up Schedule

Start reduced, climb back to full over 8 weeks, then keep progressing. **Only advance a phase if the symptom gate stayed green through the prior phase.** If the elbows flare, repeat the previous phase — the timeline is a guide, the tissue is the boss.

| Phase | Weeks | What you're doing | Roughly |
|-------|-------|-------------------|---------|
| **1 – Base** | 1–2 | Run the **starting doses** as listed in each day. Lock in the **daily elbow habit**. Day 3 finisher OFF. Groove form on all plyos. | ~70–75% of full |
| **2 – Build** | 3–4 | Add **1 set** to the main lifts (rows, presses, swings toward target). Plyos: add 1 rep per set. Finishers to 3 rounds. **Introduce Day 3 finisher at 1 round.** | ~80–85% |
| **3 – Develop** | 5–6 | Reach **target sets/reps** on most lifts. Plyos at target reps. Day 3 finisher to 2 rounds. Med-ball throws/slams to target. | ~90–95% |
| **4 – Peak** | 7–8 | Full **target loads** (the original plan). Day 3 finisher to 3 rounds. Hammer curls may move from moderate → moderate-heavy *if* gate stays green. | 100% |
| **Beyond** | 9+ | Normal progression: every 4 weeks add **5–10% load** to KB/DB lifts, OR a rep to power sets, OR reduce rest — **one variable at a time, not all at once.** | 100%+ |

**Deload:** Every ~4th week (or any time symptoms flare or sleep/energy dip), drop all loads ~20% for a week, then resume. Planned easy weeks prevent the overuse spiral — especially with a tendon history.

**Elbow note:** The daily elbow protocol does **not** ramp — it runs at full dose (the 3×15s above) from Day 1 and continues regardless of workout phase. Frequency is the active ingredient.

## Key Principles

**Elbow recovery is daily and cumulative.** The Tyler Twist program takes 6–8 weeks of consistent *daily* work. Do it every day, on a fixed cue, separate from workouts. Don't skip it because the elbows feel okay.

**Symptom gate over schedule.** Pain ≤3/10 during and no worse next morning = progress. Worse than that = hold or drop 20%. The 8-week timeline bends to the tissue, not the other way around.

**Explosive work = max intent, full rest.** Rest fully between power reps (jumps, bounds, throws). Tired power training just trains you to move slowly under fatigue. This is why the plyo volume starts low and climbs slowly.

**Grip tension protects the elbow.** Avoid white-knuckling kettlebells and dumbbells. A relaxed grip with just enough tension to control the weight cuts forearm stress significantly.

**On-court connection.** The split step, lateral bounds, and rotational medicine ball work should feel directly connected to match play. If you're playing, prioritize Day 3 the day before a match and Day 4 after.
