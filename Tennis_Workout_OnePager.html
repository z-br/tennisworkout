Dynamic Tennis Workout

4 days/week · ~45 min · KB · TRX · DB · Bands · Bar
★ Daily~7 min
  • 1. Tyler Twist — 3×15
  • 2. Reverse Tyler Twist — 3×15
  • 3. Pronation/Supination — 2×15 ea
  • 4. Wrist Extensor Stretch — 3×30s
  • 5. Finger Extension band — 3×20
Symptom gate: pain ≤3/10 & no worse next morning → progress. Worse → hold or drop 20%.

Day 1Lower Body Power + Lateral Quickness

Warm-up 10 min: forearm prep, lateral shuffles, 90/90 work, band walks, split steps
  1. KB Swings 3×12 → 4×15
  2. Bulgarian Split Squat 2×8 → 3×8 ea
  3. Lateral Bounds (skater) 2×4 → 3×6 ea
  4. TRX Squat + Jump 3 → 4 sets (3 slow + 1 jump)
  5. Calf Raises (towel under toes) 3×10 ea · single-leg @ ~30 lb · slow 3-2-3
Finisher · Agility ladder 3 → 5 rounds

Day 2Upper Body Strength + Rotational Power

Warm-up 10 min: band pull-aparts, shoulder CARs, arm swings, band external rotation
  1. Pull-Up Progression (band-assist) 2×5 → 3×6–8 · neutral/pronated · also 2–3×/wk light
  2. 1-Arm DB Row 2×10 → 3×10 ea
  3. DB Shoulder Press 2×10 → 3×10
  4. Med Ball Rotational Throw 3×6 → 4×8 ea
  5. Hammer Curls (moderate) 2×8 → 3×8
  6. AxisBoard Single-Leg Balance 2×20–30s ea → 45s / eyes closed

Day 3Full Body Explosive + Agility

Warm-up 10 min: forearm prep, fast feet, world's greatest stretch
  1. KB Clean + Press 2×5 → 3×5 ea
  2. Broad Jump 2×4 → 4×5
  3. Med Ball Overhead Slam 2×6 → 3×8
  4. Push-Up (progress levels) 2×8 → 3×8
  5. Lateral Shuffle + Split Step 3 rounds · Weeks 1–2 only
  6. Calf Raises (towel under toes) 3×10 ea · single-leg @ ~30 lb · slow 3-2-3
Finisher (wk 3+, replaces #5) · 3 rds: 10 swings · 5 broad jumps · 10 split steps

Day 4Core, Stability + Hip Mobility

Warm-up 5 min: foam roll, cat-cow, thread the needle
  1. TRX Plank Variations 2×30s → 3×30s
  2. Suitcase Carry 3×30–40s ea · heavy, relaxed grip
  3. Band Chop (high → low) 3×12 ea
  4. Pallof Press (kneeling) 3×12 ea
  5. KB Windmill 3×5 ea
  6. AxisBoard Single-Leg Balance 2×20–30s ea → 45s / eyes closed
  7. Hip Mobility Circuit ×2 · 90/90, pigeon, adductor rock-back
Ramp: first number = Weeks 1–2 start · second = Weeks 7–8 target. Add ~1 set/phase every 2 weeks. Deload 20% every 4th week.
Rest 1 day between sessions · Mon / Tue / Thu / Sat · "ea" = each side · Pull-ups: also practice 2–3×/wk light (grease the groove)
