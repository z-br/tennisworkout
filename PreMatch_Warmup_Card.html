🎾 Pre-Match Warm-Up

~5 min · on court before play
  1. Achilles pogos + easy skipsRaise HR, prime calves & feet45s
  2. Leg swingsFront-back ×10 + lateral ×10 each45s
  3. Walking lunge + reach/rotate4–5 each side, twist over front leg45s
  4. Arm circles + cross-body swings + wrist rollsWake the shoulder gently30s
  5. Forearm / elbow prepWrist flex/extend; light yellow FlexBar twists (blood flow, NOT daily eccentrics); grip squeeze-and-spread30s
  6. Shuffles + 2 split steps + 2 short sprintsMove in your playing planes45s
  7. Shadow swings5 FH · 5 BH · 5 serves — slow → full60s
Then warm-up hit: build swing pace gradually — never first-ball-full-pace. Your #1 on-court elbow protector.
